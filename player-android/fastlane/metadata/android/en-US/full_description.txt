Player plays audio and video stored on your Android device or streamed from your self-hosted Player server.

Start with On this device: choose files using Android's document picker, keep a local library, and resume where you left off. No account or network connection is needed. Audio keeps playing in the background with notification and lock-screen controls. Removing an entry leaves the original file on your device. Supported formats depend on your device's decoders.

Connect to your Player server to browse sets, listen to audio, watch videos, and open public shares. Server playback progress syncs, including updates queued while your device is offline. Local playback history stays on your device.

Source code and releases: https://github.com/snonux/player
