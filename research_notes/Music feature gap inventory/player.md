# Player source feature inventory and music gaps

## What does Player currently implement?

### Takeaway
Player already implements a broad file-based media library, playback, account data, lyrics, Android offline files and multi-server support. It does not yet have a music catalogue (artists/albums/tag metadata), playlists, or a real editable music queue. Android is currently a single-file player; the web has automatic browse-order advance.
